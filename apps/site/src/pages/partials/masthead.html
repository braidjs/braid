Microfrontends, made safe

The safe microfrontend orchestrator.

Braid puts apps from different teams on one page. It untangles the libraries they'd normally have to share, and it catches the moment two apps stop agreeing on their data, before your users do.

  • Safe.Each app runs in its own sealed space. It can't break the page or the apps beside it.
  • Untangled.No shared dependency graph. Every app brings its own libraries, at its own versions.
  • Skew-aware.Apps check they agree on data. Old data gets upgraded, and real mismatches fail loudly.
