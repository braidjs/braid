What we
promise

Safe, untangled, and honest about the rest.

Your page stays yours.

Braid never changes anything global on your page. We have a whole set of tests whose only job is to fail if it ever does.

No more version standoffs.

Each app keeps its own libraries. One team can upgrade React this week without waiting on, or breaking, anyone else.

Mismatches get caught, not shipped.

When apps meet, they check that they agree on their data. Older data gets upgraded step by step. If it can't be, you get a clear reason instead of a quietly wrong screen.

"Safe" means what it says.

By default, apps your company owns are kept apart from each other. For code from outside your company, trust="untrusted" walls it off completely, cookies and storage included.

Corrections & clarifications

We write down everything that goes wrong: what you'll see, why it happens, and how to fix it. A few favourites:

  • “The app is always one page behind.”
  • “It shows up, flickers, and then there are two of it.”
  • “The slot is empty and there are no errors.”
  • “One app's saved data stops loading after another app deploys.”
